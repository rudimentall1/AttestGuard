AttestGuard

An AI agent watches for trade-finance deliveries and proposes advances. It cannot move a single unit of money on its own — a separate, boring, deterministic contract decides that.

Live on Creditcoin CC3 testnet

The rule

Attestcoin Protocol proves that a delivery event really happened on another chain — no oracle, nothing to trust on that front. A separate contract on Creditcoin then checks the request against fixed limits: within the supplier's cap, it pays out automatically. Above it, or with a brand-new buyer relationship, a human has to confirm it first. The AI in the middle only watches and submits proofs. It has no path to funds that skips this contract.

Proven on-chain, not just in tests
